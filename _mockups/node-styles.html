Node styles

Five looks for the nodes, all drawn on the same hero graph so the only difference is the style. Drag any node, and the wires follow. Whichever you pick would apply to the hero and the contact graph.

1 Unreal Blueprint

The editor your clients already know. Colored gradient headers by node type, triangle pins for execution flow, round pins for data.

  • Reads instantly as game tech
  • Busiest of the five

2 Glass

Frosted panels over the headset photo, soft glowing pins and wires.

  • Most premium and atmospheric
  • Needs a photo or gradient behind it, so it suits the hero best

3 Technical drawing

Outlined nodes on the blue blueprint grid, mono labels, square pins and a hard offset shadow.

  • Matches the new About grid
  • Wires are thin white, so the colored pins carry the meaning less

4 Soft cards

Smooth dark cards with an icon chip in the header, divider lines, ring pins and wires that fade from one pin color to the next.

  • Cleanest and most modern
  • Closest to today's design tools

5 Light paper

White cards with tinted headers on a pale dotted grid. A bright counterpoint to the dark hero.

  • Would make the contact section a light band
  • Breaks from the dark theme, so a bigger change

Reply with a number, or mix them, for example "4 for the nodes and 2 for the hero background".
